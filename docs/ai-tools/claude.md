# Claude at Syracuse

> Sample/placeholder content for the demo build.

Syracuse University provides access to Claude through Enterprise Claude for
eligible members of the university community. Claude can help with writing,
analysis, coding, and answering questions grounded in university documentation.

## Who can use it

Eligibility varies by role and, in some cases, by program or department.
If you are unsure whether your account includes Claude, ask ITS or check the
access page.

## What data is allowed

Follow university data-handling policy. Do not enter regulated data
(for example FERPA-protected records) into any AI tool unless the tool and
the use have been explicitly approved for that data class.

## Requesting access

Access is managed through your university identity. See the access
documentation for current steps.
