# ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot

> Sample/placeholder content for the demo build.

Syracuse provides enterprise access to Microsoft Copilot for many faculty and
staff through existing Microsoft 365 licensing. Availability of other tools
such as ChatGPT depends on affiliation and, in some cases, on the specific
software bundle your school or department provides.

## Licensing differs by affiliation

Some programs include access to tools that others do not. If a tool you need
is not available to you, ITS can tell you whether your affiliation is eligible
or whether a request is required.

## Data handling

The same university data policy applies across all AI tools. When in doubt
about whether a data class is permitted, ask before entering it.
